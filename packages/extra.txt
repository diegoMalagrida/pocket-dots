# Packages that on the original laptop arrived as a DEPENDENCY of something else
# (so they do not show up in `pacman -Qqen`) but that the rice or the installer
# itself calls by name. If they are not installed deliberately, on a new machine
# they might never turn up at all.

# --- fonts the configs ask for by name ---
ttf-jetbrains-mono-nerd
ttf-nerd-fonts-symbols-common

# --- used by install.sh / diagnose, and NOT present in a minimal Arch ---
# cmp (comparing the /etc files before overwriting them)
diffutils
# fc-cache and fc-list (registering the fonts, and checking them in diagnose)
fontconfig

# --- services from services.txt that only ever arrived by accident ---
# These two DO get installed anyway as a dependency of something else, so they
# fix no bug today. They are listed explicitly because a package that exists
# only as a dependency becomes an orphan the moment whatever pulled it in goes
# away, and a `pacman -Rns $(pacman -Qtdq)` would take it out without warning:
#   nftables      -> needed by nftables.service, and without it /etc/nftables.conf
#                    (the firewall that ships in system/etc) is dead weight
#   xdg-user-dirs -> needed by xdg-user-dirs.service, which is what creates
#                    ~/Pictures; without that folder there is nowhere to put the
#                    wallpapers
nftables
xdg-user-dirs
